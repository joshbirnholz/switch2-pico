# Notes for Claude

## Writing UI text

- Describe things that are about to happen in the future tense, the way
  people write UI text: "The dongle will restart, and then you'll need to
  reconnect your controller." Never the present tense for future events
  ("The dongle restarts and your controller reconnects by itself.").
- Only promise what actually happens: after a dongle restart or firmware
  update the controller has to be reconnected; it doesn't come back by itself.
