Connect your Switch2-Pico

Plug in the dongle, click Connect over USB and choose it. Its name follows its mode: "Pro Controller", "DualSense Edge Wireless Controller", "DualSense Wireless Controller", "Switch2-Pico Xbox 360 Controller", "WUP-028" (GameCube adapter), "Switch2-Pico SInput" or "Switch2-Pico (no controller)".

Each mode is a separate USB device to the browser, so pick it once per mode; after that the page connects by itself. Needs Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser. Linux: install the udev rule from the README first. If the dongle is set to appear only while a controller is connected, connect a controller first.

Profiles

Hold C + Home until you hear a click, then press a button to activate its profile. Drag a profile onto another button to swap them.A single Joy-Con 2 has no shortcut: choose the profile in use here.
Click the button to copy to.

Hold C and either GL or GR, and then press another button to map it to the back buttons. Do it again to clear the mapping.

Try it under Tools → Haptics test.

Mouse Mode is only available for emulated Xbox 360 and SInput controllers.

Lay a Joy-Con 2 on its side on a surface and move it: it's a mouse while it lies there, and each Joy-Con is its own mouse. Its shoulder button clicks, its trigger right-clicks, its stick click middle-clicks and its stick scrolls; picked up, they're the controller's again. In Xbox 360 and SInput profiles of the Joy-Con 2 the dongle has two USB mice next to the controller, so turning Mouse Mode on or off takes effect right away.

Decky plugin

These options (such as mapping a button to the Quick Access Menu) need the Switch2-Pico plugin for Decky Loader, and only work on a Steam Deck or other SteamOS device in Game Mode. To get it, install Decky Loader from decky.xyz, then in Decky’s settings turn on Developer mode and choose Install Plugin from URL with https://joshbirnholz.github.io/switch2-pico/decky/Switch2-Pico.zip. Once you use the plugin, this will be turned on for you.
