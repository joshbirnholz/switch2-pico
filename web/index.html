Switch2-Pico

… USB …
Connect to your dongle over USB. Plug in the Switch2-Pico, click Connect over USB and choose "Pro Controller". Needs Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser. Linux: install the udev rule from the README first. If WebUSB is turned off on the dongle, use the Wi-Fi page instead (tap BOOTSEL, join Switch2-Pico-XXXX, open http://192.168.4.1).

Controller

To pair: hold the small Sync button on the Switch 2 controller until the lights sweep. The dongle pairs automatically and remembers it; afterwards any button press wakes and reconnects it.

USB and system

Mode shortcut

On the controller, hold C + Home until its lights blink, then press a button below to switch to that mode (the dongle restarts). C + Home again, or a few seconds without pressing anything, leaves without a change. Buttons set to – are ignored; with every button set to – the shortcut is off.

Button mapping

Choose what each physical button sends to the host, in the current USB mode (each mode keeps its own mapping). The drawing shows the controller live: pressed buttons light up and the sticks move.

The Switch 2 rumble frequency encoding is not fully documented. If effects feel off, try "Fixed" or adjust the scale.

Haptics test

Plays feedback on the connected controller. The effects are the ones the shortcuts use; the built-in samples are stored in the controller (number 1 is the old remap buzz).

Firmware update

The dongle restarted as a USB drive (NICENANO, FTHR840BOOT or RP2350). Choose that drive and the firmware is written to it; the dongle installs it and restarts by itself.
